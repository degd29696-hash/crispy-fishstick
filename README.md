🌋 MOUNT BATUR & KINTAMANI

Kintamani is a beautiful mountain area in Bali, famous for Mount Batur and Lake Batur. The area offers cool weather and beautiful natural scenery.

Mount Batur is approximately 1,717 meters high and is part of the Batur UNESCO Global Geopark.

Visitors can enjoy mountain views, the lake, volcanic landscapes, and various natural and cultural attractions around Kintamani.
